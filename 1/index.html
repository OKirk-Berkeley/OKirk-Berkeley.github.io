First Attempt: NCC over the Whole Image.

Images in this collection are digital scans of black-and-white exposures taken on glass plates taken using red, blue, and green filters. To align the different colorways of the image, I matched the red and green colorways to the blue by exauhstively searching a 15 by 15 pixel displacement. To determine how well each displacement fit, I used Normalized Cross-Correlation (NCC). To do this, I treated each image as a 1D vector, and subtracted its mean and normalized it. I then compared the dot products of the two vectors. In my first attempt, I took the NCC over the whole image, including the misaligned color boarders. These boarders overcontributed to the final calculated alignment, resulting in these misaligned images.
